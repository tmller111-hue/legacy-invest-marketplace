Process · Eight stages

How the Work Gets Done

The rehabilitation is executed in a fixed sequence by our in-house construction division. Every stage produces a record — a photo set, a permit, an inspection result or a signed checklist — and those records are what back the warranties on the final page.

01
Condition assessment
The property is walked and photographed in full. Every deficiency is documented with a dated photo before a dollar is spent.
02
Scope written and priced
This document is produced — a line-item scope by trade, priced from our own construction division rather than a third-party bid.
03
Permits pulled
Permits are acquired in every jurisdiction that requires them. We do not perform permit-required work without one.
04
Before photos
A complete before set is captured and filed so the finished condition can be verified against the starting condition.
05
Rehab executed
Trades are sequenced: demo and trashout, roof and exterior dry-in, mechanical rough-in, drywall, paint, flooring, cabinets and fixtures, punch list.
06
Trade inspections
Plumbing, electrical, HVAC and building inspections are scheduled and passed where the jurisdiction requires them.
07
Quality control walkthrough
An internal QC checklist is walked and signed off by construction management before the job is called complete.
08
Documentation and handoff
After photos are filed, warranties are activated and the property is turned over rent-ready to property management.
What you receive at closing — the final scope of work with every line marked complete, the before-and-during-and-after photo set, the signed quality control checklist, and the written warranties described on the last page of this document.
Section A · Roofing and attic

Specification by Trade

 Roofing & Attic SECTION A
The roof is settled first, because everything downstream depends on the house being dry. A roof either gets replaced outright or gets inspected, repaired and certified with documented remaining life. We do not hand over a property with an unresolved roof.
Item & Legacy Invest standard specification
Roof covering  — Tear off to decking, replace bad sheathing, synthetic underlayment, architectural shingles.
Flashing & boots  — New pipe boots, valley and step flashing, drip edge at all eaves and rakes.
Repair alternative  — Roofs with documented remaining life are repaired and certified in writing, not replaced.
Attic insulation  — Insulation replaced or upgraded where needed.
Ventilation  — Ridge, static or turbine venting installed if needed and balanced against soffit intake.
Attic access  — Hatch or pull-down insulated and weatherstripped.

Specification by Trade

 Exterior Envelope SECTION B
Siding, trim, glazing and gutters are treated as one system. The objective is a sealed, painted, water-shedding exterior that will not generate a maintenance call in your first two years of ownership.

Specification by Trade

 Heating & Air Conditioning SECTION C
HVAC is evaluated by a licensed technician, not by eye. Systems past economical repair are replaced with a new matched system rather than patched, and that replacement is what the mechanical warranty attaches to.
Item & Legacy Invest standard specification
System  — Evaluated by a licensed contractor; replaced with a new matched system if beyond economical repair.

Condenser  — New level pad and locking condenser cage installed as a theft deterrent.
Commissioning  — Refrigerant charge verified and temperature split documented at startup.
Vent cleaning  — Full duct and vent cleaning performed before turnover.

Section D · Plumbing

Specification by Trade

 Plumbing SECTION D
Supply and drain systems are tested under pressure and flow rather than assumed. Anything that leaks, drains slowly or is made of an obsolete material is corrected before the walls close.
Item & Legacy Invest standard specification
Supply lines  — System pressure tested; leaking, corroded or galvanized sections replaced.
Drain, waste & vent  — Flow tested and cleared; main line scoped where age or condition warrants.
Water heater  — Serviced or replaced; new T&P valve and discharge line; pan and drain where required.
Fixtures  — New faucets, supply lines, angle stops, toilets and wax rings throughout.
Exterior  — Hose bibs replaced; main shutoff located, tagged and confirmed operable.
Documentation  — Twist-drain service completed and logged in the pre-closing file.

Section I · What backs the work

Turnover, Verification & Warranty

 Site, Safety & Turnover SECTION I
The last two weeks are about handing over a property that is clean, secure and photographable — the condition a tenant actually walks into on move-in day.
Item & Legacy Invest standard specification
Trashout  — All interior and exterior debris, abandoned property and construction waste removed.
Landscaping  — Overgrowth cut back, limbs cleared off the roof and service lines, beds cleaned and edged.
Grading & drainage  — Soil pulled back from the foundation and negative grading corrected.
Fencing & gates  — Repaired and made operable where present.
Rekey & secure  — All exterior doors rekeyed at completion and a lockbox set for management.
Final clean  — Full detail clean including vents, appliances, glass and fixtures, ready for photos.

Nothing in this scope is sold on our word alone. Each of the three commitments below is tied to a document that travels with the property at closing.

Permitted & Inspected
Permits are pulled and final inspections passed in every jurisdiction that requires them.
1-Year Mechanical Warranty
Twelve months from the date of closing on the systems that matter — heating and cooling, plumbing, electrical and water heater — covering parts and labor on the work performed under this scope.
90-Day SOW Warranty
Ninety days from the date of closing on the completed rehabilitation itself. If a line item in this document was not performed to specification, we return and correct it at our cost.
What the warranty does not cover
  • Tenant-caused damage, misuse, neglect or lack of routine maintenance after turnover.
  • Normal cosmetic wear, settlement cracking, and consumable items such as filters, bulbs and batteries.
  • Any line item marked N/A or deferred by written agreement, and any component outside the scope described in this document.
  • Damage from storms, flooding, freeze events or other causes properly covered by property insurance.
  • Work performed by parties other than Legacy Invest or its subcontractors after the date of closing.
